Delete your Homily account

You can delete your Homily cloud account without installing or opening the app.

Delete your account

Open the secure deletion page, sign in with the email address and password for your Homily account, then review and confirm the deletion. If you forgot your password, the page can send a reset code to your account email.

Open secure deletion page

What deletion affects

When account deletion is completed, the live cloud account, cloud sermons, and hosted review links are removed. Review links stop working. Sermons on your phones and any files you exported remain on your devices; save what you want to keep before deleting your account.

Older copies can remain in protected backups after the live account is deleted. Server and encrypted offsite daily database copies follow a 30-day retention policy. The server retains seven daily recovery snapshots and one encrypted recovery image until a newer image is verified and replaces it. These recovery copies are not removed at the moment you delete your account.

Need help?

Email homilysupport@gmail.com if you cannot access your account or need help. We will need to verify account ownership before acting on a support request. Do not send your password, verification code, sermon text, or backup files.
